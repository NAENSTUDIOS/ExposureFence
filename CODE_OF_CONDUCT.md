# ExposureFence Community Standards & Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

The ExposureFence project is dedicated to fostering an open, welcoming, professional, and productive environment for all contributors, maintainers, and community members.

Whether you are participating in issue discussions, submitting pull requests, conducting security research, or presenting the platform, we commit to treating every participant with professional respect, fairness, and dignity.

---

## Expected Standards of Behavior

We expect all contributors to demonstrate the following behaviors:

1. **Professional Collaboration:** Communicate with empathy, clarity, and respect. Constructive disagreement and technical debates are welcomed when focused on architectural merit and evidence.
2. **Intellectual Honesty:** Adhere to our principle of transparent disclosure. Report test metrics, implementation readiness, and limitations truthfully without exaggerating or fabricating capabilities.
3. **Inclusive Participation:** Welcome new contributors, students, and engineers from all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.
4. **Safety & Security Stewardship:** Handle security inquiries and potential vulnerability reports with responsibility and discretion, following responsible disclosure practices.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviors

The following conduct is unacceptable within our community, repositories, and communication channels:

- Harassment, derogatory commentary, offensive language, or personal attacks.
- Dismissive or hostile treatment of questions from junior contributors or students.
- Deliberately misleading claims regarding test metrics, cloud spend, or security assurances.
- Unsolicited commercial promotion or spam.
- Publishing private communications without consent.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Community members who experience or observe unacceptable behavior are encouraged to report it directly to the repository maintainers:

- **Contact:** `community@naenstudios.com` (or open a confidential inquiry with repository owners).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality. Maintainers reserve the right to edit, close, or remove issues, pull requests, or comments that violate these standards, and to restrict access to individuals engaging in persistent misconduct.
